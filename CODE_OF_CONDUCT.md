# DeafBench Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DeafBench is an accessibility project. We are committed to making participation
welcoming and safe for Deaf, hard-of-hearing, disabled, and hearing contributors
of every experience level and background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when discussing ideas or code.
- Make room for different communication styles and accessibility needs.
- Use accessible language and provide text alternatives when practical.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep benchmark claims tied to reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate misgendering, ableist remarks, or dismissing accessibility needs.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing customer audio, transcripts, credentials, or other sensitive data.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or knowingly misleading benchmark claims.

## Scope

This code applies in the DeafBench repository and in public spaces where someone
is representing DeafBench. GitHub's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service
also apply to all activity hosted on GitHub.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's report and moderation controls for repository content. For threats,
harassment, disclosure of private information, or other GitHub policy concerns,
use [GitHub's abuse-reporting form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not put sensitive evidence in a public issue or discussion.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, harmful, or inconsistent with this code. Enforcement decisions will
consider context, impact, and whether the behavior is repeated.
